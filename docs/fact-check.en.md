# The 176 milestones and their fact-check

Every milestone in the film, in order of appearance. The source of truth is [`src/milestones.js`](../src/milestones.js); this table is generated from it.

**How facts were checked**: before release, entries were compared against the corresponding English Wikipedia articles (Timeline of historic inventions, Timeline of scientific discoveries, and the individual articles).

| Status | Meaning | Count |
|---|---|---|
| **Corrected** | Changed after checking | 7 |
| Checked | Checked, unchanged | 44 |
| Judgment call | Sources differ; a choice was made | 2 |
| Sourced | Based on news reports | 1 |
| Not checked | Mostly well-known modern dates, but not checked article by article | 122 |

The location is where the event happened, was completed or was found. Ancient dates are mostly approximate. Wars and disasters are left out.

Found an error? Please [open an issue](https://github.com/DDChen666/civilization-film/issues) with a source.

## Dimensions of the wonders

The wonder scenes are modeled at real scale. These are the checked figures:

| Wonder | Year | Dimensions |
|---|---|---|
| Great Pyramid (Khufu) | 2560 BC | Base 230.3 m, height 146.6 m |
| Stonehenge | 2500 BC | Outer circle 30 m across. Drawn complete; much of it is gone today |
| Etemenanki, Babylon | 600 BC | 91 m square base, 91 m tall, seven tiers (per the Esagila tablet; modern scholars think it was probably lower). No source was found for the height of each tier |
| Parthenon | 447–432 BC | 69.5 × 30.9 m, 8 × 17 columns |
| Colosseum | AD 80 | 189 × 156 m, 48 m tall |
| Angkor Wat | 1113–1150 | Central tower 65 m, moat 190 m wide |
| Temple of Heaven (Hall of Prayer) | 1420 | 38 m tall, 36 m across |
| Taj Mahal | 1653 | 73 m tall overall, plinth 95.5 m square. The plinth is actually 6 m high; the model makes it 7 m |
| Eiffel Tower | 1889 | 300 m (330 m with antennas), base 125 m |
| Saturn V | 1969 | 111 m tall |
| International Space Station | 1998 | 109 m long, orbit c. 413–422 km |

**Illustrative, not real**: the camp and village, Babylon's streets, the modern city, the chip, the paths of trade routes and sea lanes, the routes of railways and cables, and the Great Wall's terrain.
